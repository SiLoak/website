About SiLoak

Making Responsible Disposal Easier for Everyone.

SiLoak is a digital platform designed to make the collection and responsible handling of unwanted goods more accessible. Through convenient pickup services and nearby drop-off points, SiLoak helps users give their unused items a new purpose while encouraging responsible resource use.

Convenient Disposal

Make responsible disposal more convenient through doorstep scheduling and local neighborhood hubs.

Circular Management

Encourage reuse, recycling, and circular resource management to divert materials from landfills.

Transparent Tracking

Track submissions, verified weights, rewards, and donations as the service grows.
